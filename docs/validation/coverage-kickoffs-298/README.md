# Deep coverage, kickoffs and on-field presentation — Build 298

Application source: 59d1cae; smoke correction: 49f9b74 on game-day-2-test. Main stays unmerged. Earlier runs were superseded before delivery to align the landscape midfield camera and correct receiving-team notifications.

## Changes

Deep-pass DB animation previously used statistical gain for its endpoint. On incompletions this meant little or no downfield movement while WR route art continued vertically. Non-rushing coverage players now carry actual receiver route tracks; man follows the receiver laterally while zone retains zone leverage. Pursuit starts from the coverage position at the catch instead of a static starting spot. Rushers retain their blocking/pursuit behavior. Corner matching for outside receivers uses lane proximity rather than cycling through the coverage array. Explicit Man/Zone assignments take priority over coverage names; Cover 0/1 and Cover 2 Man are no longer treated as zone simply because their names contain Cover. Hail Mary retains deep-pass difficulty but its extra blanket incompletion penalty is reduced when the engine sees separation. Inaccurate throws remain possible.

Kickoffs use dedicated receiving/coverage units spread across the field. Blockers, coverage players, the kicker and returner have movement tracks. Mirrored coordinates use receiving-team field position rather than clamping a world-X formation to one side. Kickoff touchbacks now occur with seeded kick-power/wind influence: ball remains at its end-zone landing location during presentation, TOUCHBACK names the next spot, then the next play begins at the receiving 25. Touchbacks do not count as returns or run the game clock. Punt touchbacks display the 20-yard spot and remain at the opposite end-zone landing point during the notification.

Box score has a stronger accent background, larger bold label and 42dp minimum target. Coin toss is over a football field with team-colored captain sprites and a striped official; landscape uses a side panel and aligned midfield view, portrait uses a lower panel. Live field includes a referee and sideline officials as decorative pixel figures. These visuals do not add a new penalty decision system or full official movement AI.
