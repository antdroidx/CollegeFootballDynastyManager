# Build 293: position-based depth chart

The organizer shows one NCAA PPOS role at a time, in order QB through P. Offense, defense and special-team shortcuts lead to the role picker. The selected depth order and searchable candidates sit side by side on wide screens and stack on narrow screens. Cards show natural position, class year, availability and the calibrated OVR at the selected role. Drag/drop and arrow buttons reorder players; Assign moves a player into that group.

Players retain their natural position, attributes and career identity. Separate saved assignments set one playing position per athlete. This version does not implement simultaneous two-way depth slots. Game-day, weekly simulation, conference championships, bowls, playoffs and roster-derived team profiles apply the same assignments. Replay snapshots keep their historical personnel. Injured/redshirted players are skipped in game selection; healthy same-side line backups retain their jobs. Departed players' assignments are pruned during roster normalization.

Auto Set can rebuild the whole team or sort only the selected group. It prioritizes positional OVR, adds a modest scheme-fit adjustment scaled by coordinator scheme/talent-evaluation ratings, reserves specialist/line jobs before flexible positions, and changes starter counts for the supported offensive/defensive schemes. It avoids duplicate athletes and puts unavailable players behind active candidates. Positional OVR itself never receives a staff bonus. Sorting a group does not move players out of other groups.

Kicker/punter selection distinguishes PPOS 19/20 and individual stats follow the event's actual specialist ID. Existing natural-position line ordering remains compatible until an explicit chart is assigned.
