# Pro data and save contracts

## Isolation and migrations
Pro package remains `antdroid.profootball.franchise`; college package, SQLite schema and signing are unchanged. Pro JSON saves remain in `pro-franchises-v1` with primary and two rolling backups. Schema 2 adds economic and staff records. Loading schema 1 preserves its embedded fictional league, players, game results and identity, initializes estimated finances and writes schema 2 on the next save. Unknown future versions fail explicitly. Existing franchises do not silently replace their rosters with a new source pack.

## Offline NFL snapshot
`league.json` defines 32 real clubs, divisions, colors and abbreviation badges. `fictional-league.json` retains the original pack. `nfl-snapshot.json` supplies identities, source roster status, Madden-based skills and four staff slots per club. All data are bundled; new franchises need no network access. Future generated classes are fictional.

The importer `build/import-nfl-snapshot.py CACHE` reads nflverse weekly identities and statuses, EA public numeric Madden ratings, a launch-CSV fallback and cached official club staff pages. It matches normalized exact names, labels unmatched estimates, retains GSIS/ESPN external IDs, and uses deterministic player GUIDs. Each team's latest available week is used so bye teams are present. Game-day inactive players count toward the 53-man roster; reserves and practice players are distinct. Extra practice/elevated source records are preserved as reserves rather than silently deleted. Team spelling/code aliases are normalized. The source manifest records URLs, hashes, edition, team weeks and attribution. Source caches are not runtime dependencies.

Snapshot edition: 2026 weeks 6–7; Madden 27 EA Week 6 plus launch fallback. 2,593 identities: 2,255 Madden matches and 338 explicit rating estimates. Four staff slots per team; Tampa Bay's separate DC slot remains vacant because its official page assigns defense to the head coach. Position coaches from official pages form the initial hire pool. Coach ratings and all starting contract values are game estimates. At the user’s request, badges use crisp team abbreviations (SEA, BUF, LAC/LAR, NYG/NYJ) with club colors and readable text, including the scoreboard and midfield. No raster team-logo assets are bundled.

## Player exchange v1
`PlayerExport` and `ProPlayerImport` remain shared, versioned DTO/conversion APIs. Unknown versions and duplicate identities are rejected. College attributes convert to a professional scale with seeded uncertainty. File-picker import/export and comprehensive scouting calibration remain pending; this preview does not claim otherwise.

## Franchise systems preview
Implemented: salary cap, yearly salaries/guarantees, prorated signing bonuses, accelerated dead money, offers/extensions, one offseason tag, competing user/CPU FA bids, player/pick trades, waiver priority, practice promotions/demotions, seven rounds/224 draft selections, scout budget and board, rookie contracts, UDFAs, aging/regression/retirement, morale, staff replacement and owner confidence. CPU decisions use the same transaction methods and caps, preserve draft/roster money and diversify competing free-agent valuations. A monotonic transaction clock keeps waiver claims working across the postseason week-number reset. Draft and cap decisions are available in Front office; staff moves open in the offseason. Auto fill and trim tools assist roster management. Each advance saves atomically.

These are playable Phase 3/4 foundations, not complete CBA conformance: initial real contracts, compensatory picks, complex guarantees/offsets, June 1 treatment, cap rollover, full waiver-contract assumption, practice-squad waiver exposure/elevations and full NFL multi-team tiebreak chains remain pending. The current trade dialog offers one asset per side; domain APIs support packages. User firing/job-offer career flow, individualized development plans, imported draft-class UI and multi-decade economic/statistical calibration remain pending. Owner confidence is informational in this preview. Schedule opponents are generated by the existing rotation, not imported NFL fixture dates.

## Live football
One shared engine, sprites and field renderer. Professional overtime and touchback configuration remain separate. At the user's request, rendered hash spacing stays at the college view's .30. Shared college pursuit selection and defensive destination helpers keep non-tacklers in their lanes; pro coverage uses the college coverage and pass-rush helpers instead of sending every defender to the carrier. The college formulas are unchanged. Between snaps, coach mode uses the college huddle-position helper at the current ball spot, rather than leaving old sprites behind under new field markers. Full extraction of the college-coupled viewer remains incremental; specialized kick/turnover choreography and all camera presentation parity require further work.

## Validation scope
The automated economic stress test simulates 20 complete seasons and offseasons (6,400 generated prospects and 4,480 draft picks), checking roster bounds, nonnegative cap room, unique identities, aging, championships and draft completion. This verifies continuity and basic invariants; it does not establish NFL statistical or economic calibration. Separate tests cover data completeness, legacy save migration, bid competition, dead money, pick ownership, staff windows and defensive pursuit. College regression has 678 passing cases; Android workflows validate actual builds and device flows.


## October 26 appearance reference overlay

`build/import-player-appearance.py` derives cheek colors from public NFL/ESPN roster headshot URLs in the nflverse roster feed. OpenCV detects a single frontal face, samples both cheeks and rejects insufficient/highly uneven patches. `nfl-appearances.json` contains 2,248 usable source references, including 1,944 matching the bundled 2,593-player snapshot; raw photos are not shipped and the game needs no network. A small contact sheet was visually reviewed. These are photo-lighting-dependent approximations, not verified complexion measurements. Unmatched players retain editable illustrated appearances. Names/ethnicity are never used to assign colors.

The additive `SkinColorHex` supplies the same validated RGB color to portraits and shared live sprites; the seven-tone editor remains available. `AppearanceOrigin=custom` and legacy explicit skin edits take precedence over the photo overlay. College defaults and imported NCAA appearance codes retain their existing interpretation. Users can override any inferred appearance and the override persists.
